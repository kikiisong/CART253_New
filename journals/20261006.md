# Reflective Journel
## 2026.10.06
For Prototype 1, I wanted to do a character selection screen. I took it as an opportunity to test out how 3D models work in p5.js. I thought milk carton would be a fun starting point. Some of the challenges:
- Apply different textures/colors to different faces of a box. I had to draw the box as individual faces with vertices so I can assign textures individually.
- Having 3D and 2D elements at the same time. At one point, the arrows (next/previous buttons) would turn and move as I interact with the 3D milk carton...I solved it with resetting the camera and pespective before I draw the arrows.

For Prototype 2 & 3, I wanted to test out game mechanics. I didn't get to do everything I have envisioned, but I made sure to have one mechanic ready for future development, maybe I can continue in the next week...
- For prototype 2 (gacha machine), I wanted to add animation to simulate the machines in real life
- For prototype 3 (mushroom), I initially wanted to do a catcher game - a girl trying to catch good mushrooms and avoid poisonous mushrooms. I tend to take my sweet time with my 1st prototype every week and rush through the rest... so I ended up doing a RPG game mechanic (which is just moving and collision detection).
